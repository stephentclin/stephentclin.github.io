# Why I am building an industrial AI portfolio

I want this portfolio to show more than a list of technologies. The goal is to show how I think about production software, especially when software connects with factories, machines, data pipelines, and users who need reliable tools.

## What I want to demonstrate

- Building interfaces that make complex workflows easier to operate
- Designing APIs around real-time production data
- Applying AI where latency, accuracy, and reliability matter
- Turning legacy workflows into systems that teams can actually adopt

## What I will write about

This blog will collect short engineering notes, study logs, and project write-ups. Some posts will focus on full-stack implementation. Others will focus on AI, cybersecurity, automation, or lessons from hardware-software integration.

The best portfolio is not just a gallery. It is a trail of decisions.
