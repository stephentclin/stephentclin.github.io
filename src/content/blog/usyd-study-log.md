# Study log: connecting computer science with industrial systems

My current study focus is Computer Science at the University of Sydney, with an emphasis on Data Science & AI and Cybersecurity.

This direction connects naturally with my earlier work in semiconductor automation and industrial software. Production systems need more than clean code. They need reliable data flows, secure deployment practices, observability, and models that can be trusted in real environments.

## Current learning themes

- Data Science & AI for prediction, classification, and decision support
- Cybersecurity for safer software and infrastructure design
- Full-stack engineering for building usable systems around technical models
- Control systems and signal processing as a bridge between physical machines and software

Writing study notes publicly helps me turn learning into reusable engineering judgment.
